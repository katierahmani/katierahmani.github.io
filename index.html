How I work / 02

Thoughtful research.
Useful outcomes.

Good people practices are built by asking the right questions, using evidence responsibly, and making the answer useful to the people who need it. My approach brings rigor and empathy into the same room.

Start with the contextUnderstand the people, the work, and the question before choosing a solution.
Use evidence wellBring the right mix of research and data to make decisions more grounded.
Make it actionableTurn complex findings into clear recommendations people can put into practice.
Keep people in viewConsider fairness, access, and lived experience throughout the process.
